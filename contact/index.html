What I am open to

  • Workshops for engineering teams. From chat to coding agent: a half-day or full-day remote workshop for 5 to 20 developers. Each developer hands a real ticket to a coding agent, and the team sets up the checks that stop bad work before it reaches main. Flat fee per team.
  • Podcasts. Interviews about AI coding agents: checking their work, what they cost, and running several at once. Past episode: Koshelek Team, “From Zimbabwe to Russia: how a foreigner built a successful IT career” (in Russian).
  • Talks. Conference and meetup talks on the same topics, with live demos of my open-source tools. I am a former club president of a Toastmasters club in Saint Petersburg.

What to include in your message

  • For a workshop: team size, the languages and repos the team works in, and which coding agents (if any) they use today.
  • For a podcast or talk: the show or event, the audience, the date, and the topic you have in mind.
  • Your time zone.
